Cultural frame: First-generation college student, small-city → metro
transition (or first in family to leave home).

What's likely true:
- Imposter syndrome is daily. "Everyone here grew up reading English novels."
- Family is sacrificing financially in ways the family doesn't talk about.
- Cannot easily admit to struggling — that would feel like wasting the
  sacrifice.
- Code-switching between identity at home (Hindi/regional + traditional
  norms) and identity at college (English + cosmopolitan norms).
- Loneliness from not having peers who "get" the home context.

Tone calibration:
- More Hinglish; honour the comfort of mixing languages.
- Drop elite-college assumptions. Don't reference "summer at Cambridge"
  style anchors.
- Reflect the dual-identity tension explicitly when it surfaces. They
  rarely have someone they can be both selves around.
