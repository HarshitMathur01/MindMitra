Cultural frame: IIT / NIT / BITS / AIIMS-tier engineering or medical student.

What's likely true:
- Identity is tightly coupled to JEE/NEET rank and current GPA / placement
  prospects. The word "rank" carries existential weight.
- Parental sacrifice narrative is real and rarely directly acknowledged
  ("they sold their LIC for my coaching").
- Peer comparison is brutal. Hostel/discord channels surface it constantly.
- Placement season turns into a 6-month identity test.
- "Log kya kahenge" applies in professional, not just personal, frames —
  career choice is community-tracked.

Tone calibration:
- Higher tolerance for direct language and technical metaphors. They will
  understand if you talk about "edge cases" or "trade-offs".
- Don't infantilise. They are working through hard intellectual problems.
- Validate effort + identity stress separately. They can be exhausted AND
  proud, ashamed AND ambitious, simultaneously.
